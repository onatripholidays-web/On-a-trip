# On A Trip Holidays — SEO Implementation

## Focus strategy
- Pilgrimage: Char Dham, Kedarnath/Do Dham, Maharashtra 3 Jyotirlingas, Gujarat Somnath-Dwarka-Nageshwar, Ujjain-Omkareshwar, Baidyanath, Puri-Konark and Kamakhya.
- Himachal: Shimla, Manali, Kasol.
- Uttarakhand: dedicated pilgrimage/mountain category.
- Himalayas: Kashmir, Ladakh, Spiti.
- South India: Kerala, Coorg & Chikmagalur, Ooty & Wayanad, Pondicherry.
- North East & Bhutan: Sikkim, Darjeeling, Gangtok, Meghalaya, Arunachal Pradesh, Tawang, Bhutan.
- Special experience: Rann of Kutch.

## Organic lead strategy
Prioritise high-intent Hyderabad queries, destination landing pages, internal links, helpful guides, clear enquiry CTAs and Google Business Profile/local SEO. Rankings and leads cannot be guaranteed immediately; SEO should be measured through Search Console, analytics and enquiry tracking.
