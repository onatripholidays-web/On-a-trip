What your itinerary can cover

Kerala is easy to tailor around interests: misty Munnar for hills, Thekkady for wildlife and nature, and Alleppey for backwaters. A well-paced first trip usually combines all three.

  • Munnar
  • Thekkady
  • Alleppey
  • Kochi
  • Vagamon
  • Kumarakom

Planning considerations

Kerala can be visited across much of the year, but season choice depends on whether you prefer cooler hill weather, backwaters, monsoon scenery or festive experiences. Check current weather before departure.

Suggested route: Hyderabad → Kochi → Munnar → Thekkady → Alleppey/Kumarakom → Kochi → Hyderabad.
