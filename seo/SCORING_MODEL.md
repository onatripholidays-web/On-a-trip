# V26 SEO Prioritization Model

Score each issue from 1–5:

## Impact
5 = affects many important pages or revenue-generating pages
4 = affects a major commercial cluster
3 = affects several useful pages
2 = limited impact
1 = cosmetic

## Confidence
5 = clearly verified
4 = strongly supported
3 = likely
2 = uncertain
1 = speculative

## Effort
5 = very easy
4 = easy
3 = moderate
2 = difficult
1 = very difficult

## Priority
Priority = Impact × Confidence × Effort

Fix high-scoring issues first.

Do not prioritize an issue merely because an SEO tool reports it. Verify whether it affects users, crawling, indexing, relevance, trust or conversions.
