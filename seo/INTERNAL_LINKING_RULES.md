# V26 Internal Linking Rules

## Commercial hub
Each major destination should have one primary commercial page.

## Supporting content
Guide/cost/best-time/packing/itinerary pages should link back to the commercial hub.

## Commercial pages
Commercial pages should link to useful supporting resources and related packages.

## Anchor text
Prefer natural descriptive anchors:
- Char Dham Yatra packages from Hyderabad
- Char Dham Yatra cost from Hyderabad
- Kedarnath Yatra guide
- Kashmir tour packages from Hyderabad

Avoid repeating the exact same anchor unnaturally on every page.

## Navigation
Important commercial pages should be reachable from:
- homepage
- destinations/packages page
- relevant category pages
- relevant guide pages

## Orphan-page rule
Every important indexable commercial or informational page should have at least one meaningful internal link pointing to it.
