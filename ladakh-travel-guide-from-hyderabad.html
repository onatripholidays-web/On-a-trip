Ladakh Travel Guide from Hyderabad: Best Time, Route, Places & Packing

Ladakh is a high-altitude destination where good planning matters as much as sightseeing. A sensible itinerary starts with acclimatisation in Leh before moving to higher valleys and passes.

Best time to visit

The main road-trip season is generally late spring through early autumn, with exact access depending on snowfall and road conditions. Winter offers a very different experience and needs specialised planning.

Suggested route from Hyderabad

Hyderabad → Leh by air → acclimatisation → local Leh sightseeing → Nubra Valley → Pangong Lake → Leh → optional Tso Moriri → Leh → Hyderabad.

Practical planning tips

  • Keep the first 24-48 hours light after arriving in Leh.
  • Hydrate, rest and avoid strenuous activity immediately after arrival.
  • Carry warm layers, sun protection and medicines prescribed for you.
  • Keep copies of ID and check current permit requirements.
  • Do not judge driving time by distance alone; mountain roads can be slow.

Frequently asked questions

How many days are enough for Ladakh?

A first Ladakh trip is best planned for about 7 to 10 nights, depending on whether Tso Moriri and long road sectors are included.

Why is acclimatisation important?

Leh is at high altitude, and allowing the body to adjust before travelling higher can reduce the risk of altitude-related illness.

Can families visit Ladakh?

Yes, provided travellers are comfortable with altitude and the itinerary is paced appropriately.
