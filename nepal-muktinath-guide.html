Nepal & Muktinath Travel Guide: Route & Planning Tips

Plan Kathmandu, Pokhara and Muktinath with route guidance, travel time, altitude considerations and useful preparation tips.

Why plan this destination carefully?

Nepal can be enjoyed in very different ways depending on season, traveller profile, route and available time. Instead of packing every attraction into one schedule, build a practical plan with realistic travel time, comfortable stays and enough flexibility for local conditions.

What to include in your itinerary

  • Choose the right arrival and departure point.
  • Keep daily driving and sightseeing realistic.
  • Confirm hotel location and room category before booking.
  • Check meals, transfers, entry tickets and optional activities.
  • Keep a buffer for weather, traffic and local operating conditions.

Best for

Families, couples, friends and private groups can customise the route according to comfort, budget and interests.

Before you book

Travel conditions can change. Final hotels, vehicle category, meal plan, permits and departure timings should always be confirmed in the final booking voucher.
