DAY-WISE PLAN

Complete Itinerary

Pickup, route, sightseeing, stay and meal flow for every day.

Day 01

Arrival in Leh • Acclimatisation

Pickup from Leh Airport and transfer to hotel. Rest and acclimatise to the high altitude. Evening at Leh Market if comfortable.

📍 Route: Leh🏨 Stay: Hotel in Leh🍽️ Meals: Breakfast / Dinner
Day 02

Leh Local + Sham Valley

Visit Hall of Fame, Gurudwara Pathar Sahib, Magnetic Hill and Sangam. Return to Leh for rest.

📍 Route: Leh🏨 Stay: Hotel in Leh🍽️ Meals: Breakfast / Dinner
Day 03

Leh → Khardung La → Nubra

Drive over Khardung La towards Nubra Valley. Visit Diskit Monastery and Hunder sand dunes; optional camel ride subject to local conditions.

📍 Route: Nubra🏨 Stay: Camp / Hotel in Nubra🍽️ Meals: Breakfast / Dinner
Day 04

Nubra → Turtuk → Nubra

Excursion to Turtuk, a scenic Balti village close to the northern frontier. Return to Nubra by evening.

📍 Route: Nubra🏨 Stay: Camp / Hotel in Nubra🍽️ Meals: Breakfast / Dinner
Day 05

Nubra → Pangong Lake

Travel via the Shyok route to Pangong Tso. Enjoy the changing colours of the lake and sunset.

📍 Route: Pangong🏨 Stay: Camp in Pangong🍽️ Meals: Breakfast / Dinner
Day 06

Pangong → Chang La → Leh

Drive back to Leh via Chang La. Evening free for shopping and leisure.

📍 Route: Leh🏨 Stay: Hotel in Leh🍽️ Meals: Breakfast / Dinner
Day 07

Departure from Leh

Pickup from hotel and transfer to Leh Airport for onward journey.

📍 Route: Leh Airport🏨 Stay: —🍽️ Meals: Breakfast
