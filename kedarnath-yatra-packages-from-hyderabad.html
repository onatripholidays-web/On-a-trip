What your itinerary can cover

Kedarnath is one of the most important pilgrimage destinations in Uttarakhand and a demanding mountain journey. Travellers from Hyderabad should plan the long-distance transfer and the final road-and-walk section separately.

  • Kedarnath Temple
  • Sonprayag
  • Gaurikund
  • Guptkashi
  • Triyuginarayan
  • Rishikesh

Planning considerations

The main pilgrimage season is generally from late spring to autumn, subject to official opening and closing announcements. May-June and September-October are popular planning windows; monsoon conditions require extra caution.

Suggested route: Hyderabad → Delhi/Haridwar/Rishikesh → Guptkashi/Sitapur → Sonprayag → Gaurikund → Kedarnath → return to the lower valley → Hyderabad.
