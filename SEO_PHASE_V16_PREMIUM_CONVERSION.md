# SEO_PHASE_V16_PREMIUM_CONVERSION

V16 builds on V15 and focuses on making destination landing pages clearer for search visitors and stronger for enquiries.

## Changes
- Added breadcrumb trail and BreadcrumbList structured data to the 22 Hyderabad destination landing pages.
- Added a compact Hyderabad-focused enquiry bar near the top of each landing page.
- Added a 3-step planning section: share dates, choose trip, confirm booking.
- Added a strong WhatsApp conversion block with current-details language instead of promising stale prices/availability.
- Added responsive mobile styling.
- Added lazy loading/async decoding to content images while keeping the logo eager.
- Preserved existing V15 imagery, SEO metadata, FAQ/schema, redirects and internal links.

## Deployment
Upload the contents of `On-a-trip-main` to the repository's `main` branch.

## QA after deployment
