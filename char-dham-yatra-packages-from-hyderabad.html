What your itinerary can cover

Char Dham Yatra is a Himalayan pilgrimage covering Yamunotri, Gangotri, Kedarnath and Badrinath. For travellers starting in Hyderabad, the key is to plan the long-distance journey, mountain transfers, stays and pilgrimage days as one connected itinerary.

  • Yamunotri
  • Gangotri
  • Kedarnath
  • Badrinath
  • Haridwar & Rishikesh

Planning considerations

May to June is popular for the opening-season pilgrimage, while late September to October can offer cooler weather and fewer crowds. Monsoon travel needs extra caution because mountain roads can be affected by heavy rain and landslides.

Suggested route: Hyderabad → Delhi/Haridwar → Barkot → Yamunotri → Uttarkashi → Gangotri → Guptkashi/Sitapur → Sonprayag → Kedarnath → Chamoli/Joshimath → Badrinath → Rishikesh/Haridwar → Hyderabad.

Char Dham Yatra from Hyderabad: important details

Before booking, compare the parts of the journey that can affect your experience: long-distance travel, mountain transfers, accommodation, meals, pilgrimage arrangements and the support available during the trip.

Travel & transfers

Ask whether your package includes the Hyderabad-to-base journey and the local Uttarakhand transfers. Confirm the joining point, vehicle type and return plan for your batch.

Stay & meals

Confirm the hotel locations, room-sharing arrangement, number of nights and meal plan. Mountain accommodation can vary by route, availability and season.

Yatra preparation

Plan for walking, altitude, changing weather and long road journeys. Senior citizens and families should review mobility needs and medical advice before travel.

Common route planning structure

Hyderabad → Delhi/Haridwar → Barkot → Yamunotri → Uttarkashi → Gangotri → Guptkashi/Sitapur → Sonprayag → Kedarnath → Chamoli/Joshimath → Badrinath → Rishikesh/Haridwar → Hyderabad. The actual sequence and overnight stops can change with road conditions and the confirmed itinerary.
