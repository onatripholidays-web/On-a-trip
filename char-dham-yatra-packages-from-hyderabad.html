What your itinerary can cover

Char Dham Yatra is a Himalayan pilgrimage covering Yamunotri, Gangotri, Kedarnath and Badrinath. For travellers starting in Hyderabad, the key is to plan the long-distance journey, mountain transfers, stays and pilgrimage days as one connected itinerary.

  • Yamunotri
  • Gangotri
  • Kedarnath
  • Badrinath
  • Haridwar & Rishikesh

Planning considerations

May to June is popular for the opening-season pilgrimage, while late September to October can offer cooler weather and fewer crowds. Monsoon travel needs extra caution because mountain roads can be affected by heavy rain and landslides.

Suggested route: Hyderabad → Delhi/Haridwar → Barkot → Yamunotri → Uttarkashi → Gangotri → Guptkashi/Sitapur → Sonprayag → Kedarnath → Chamoli/Joshimath → Badrinath → Rishikesh/Haridwar → Hyderabad.

Hyderabad to Char Dham Yatra: what your package should clarify

A good Char Dham package should clearly explain where your journey begins, how you reach the Uttarakhand base, what local transport is arranged, where you stay, what meals are included and which pilgrimage registrations or tickets are your responsibility. Before booking from Hyderabad, ask for the complete day-wise itinerary and the latest inclusions in writing.

For families and senior citizens, also confirm walking requirements, altitude-related travel, room-sharing arrangements, emergency support, luggage handling and available local assistance. If your group prefers a particular travel style, ask whether a group departure, private vehicle or customised itinerary is available for the selected dates.
