Char Dham Yatra from Hyderabad: Route, Best Time, Cost & Planning Guide

Char Dham Yatra is a Himalayan pilgrimage covering Yamunotri, Gangotri, Kedarnath and Badrinath. For travellers starting in Hyderabad, the key is to plan the long-distance journey, mountain transfers, stays and pilgrimage days as one connected itinerary.

Best time to visit

May to June is popular for the opening-season pilgrimage, while late September to October can offer cooler weather and fewer crowds. Monsoon travel needs extra caution because mountain roads can be affected by heavy rain and landslides.

Suggested route from Hyderabad

Hyderabad → Delhi/Haridwar → Barkot → Yamunotri → Uttarkashi → Gangotri → Guptkashi/Sitapur → Sonprayag → Kedarnath → Chamoli/Joshimath → Badrinath → Rishikesh/Haridwar → Hyderabad.

Practical planning tips

  • Start fitness and walking preparation several weeks before the trip.
  • Keep warm layers and rain protection even during warmer months.
  • Carry government ID, booking confirmations and required yatra registration documents.
  • Build buffer time into the itinerary for mountain traffic and weather.
  • For seniors, discuss walking pace, room location and available support before booking.

Frequently asked questions

How many days are needed for Char Dham from Hyderabad?

A road itinerary commonly needs around 10 to 14 days once the Hyderabad-to-North India travel is included. The exact duration depends on the starting point, route and number of rest nights.

Which Char Dham temples are covered?

The Uttarakhand circuit covers Yamunotri, Gangotri, Kedarnath and Badrinath.

Is Char Dham suitable for families?

Yes, but the mountain journey is physically demanding. Families should choose a comfortable pace, suitable accommodation and realistic daily travel times.
