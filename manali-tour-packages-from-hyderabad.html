What your itinerary can cover

Manali is one of Himachal Pradesh’s most versatile holiday bases, combining mountain scenery, cafes, temples, adventure activities and road-trip routes. It can be planned as a standalone holiday or paired with Shimla or Spiti.

  • Mall Road
  • Hadimba Temple
  • Solang Valley
  • Atal Tunnel
  • Old Manali
  • Sissu

Planning considerations

Spring and summer are comfortable for sightseeing, winter attracts snow seekers, and autumn can offer crisp mountain weather. High-altitude routes can have seasonal access restrictions.

Suggested route: Hyderabad → Delhi/Chandigarh → Manali → Solang/Atal Tunnel/Sissu → local sightseeing → return to Delhi/Chandigarh → Hyderabad.
