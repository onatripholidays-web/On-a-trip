Kerala Travel Guide from Hyderabad: Munnar, Thekkady & Alleppey

Kerala is easy to tailor around interests: misty Munnar for hills, Thekkady for wildlife and nature, and Alleppey for backwaters. A well-paced first trip usually combines all three.

Best time to visit

Kerala can be visited across much of the year, but season choice depends on whether you prefer cooler hill weather, backwaters, monsoon scenery or festive experiences. Check current weather before departure.

Suggested route from Hyderabad

Hyderabad → Kochi → Munnar → Thekkady → Alleppey/Kumarakom → Kochi → Hyderabad.

Practical planning tips

  • Do not pack too many attractions into one day.
  • Choose a houseboat based on route, duration and meal plan rather than photos alone.
  • Carry light cotton clothes plus a light jacket for Munnar.
  • For families, keep road journeys manageable and choose centrally located hotels.
  • If travelling during monsoon, plan for rain and flexible sightseeing.

Frequently asked questions

How many days are ideal for Kerala?

Five to seven nights is a good starting range for Munnar, Thekkady and Alleppey.

Is a houseboat stay necessary?

It is optional, but an overnight or day cruise can be a memorable way to experience the backwaters.

Can Kerala be done from Hyderabad?

Yes. Hyderabad has convenient air connectivity to Kerala gateways, making it practical for short holidays.
