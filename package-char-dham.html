DAY-WISE PLAN

Complete Itinerary

Pickup, route, sightseeing, stay and meal flow for every day.

Day 01

Delhi → Haridwar

Pickup from airport/railway station and drive to Haridwar. Evening Ganga Aarti, subject to timing.

📍 Route: Haridwar🏨 Stay: Hotel in Haridwar🍽️ Meals: Dinner
Day 02

Haridwar → Barkot

Proceed towards Barkot via Mussoorie route. Check-in and rest.

📍 Route: Barkot🏨 Stay: Hotel in Barkot🍽️ Meals: Breakfast / Dinner
Day 03

Barkot → Janki Chatti → Yamunotri → Barkot

Drive to Janki Chatti, trek/pony/palki options to Yamunotri, darshan and return.

📍 Route: Barkot🏨 Stay: Hotel in Barkot🍽️ Meals: Breakfast / Dinner
Day 04

Barkot → Uttarkashi

Drive to Uttarkashi and visit Kashi Vishwanath Temple if time permits.

📍 Route: Uttarkashi🏨 Stay: Hotel in Uttarkashi🍽️ Meals: Breakfast / Dinner
Day 05

Uttarkashi → Gangotri → Uttarkashi

Excursion to Gangotri for temple darshan and Bhagirathi river views. Return to Uttarkashi.

📍 Route: Uttarkashi🏨 Stay: Hotel in Uttarkashi🍽️ Meals: Breakfast / Dinner
Day 06

Uttarkashi → Guptkashi / Phata

Long drive towards the Kedarnath sector. Check-in and prepare for the next day.

📍 Route: Guptkashi / Phata🏨 Stay: Hotel / Guesthouse🍽️ Meals: Breakfast / Dinner
Day 07

Guptkashi / Phata → Sonprayag → Kedarnath

Transfer to Sonprayag and proceed by approved local transport/trek/heli option as booked. Kedarnath darshan and overnight stay.

📍 Route: Kedarnath🏨 Stay: Guesthouse / Camp🍽️ Meals: Breakfast / Dinner
Day 08

Kedarnath → Sonprayag → Guptkashi

Return journey from Kedarnath to Sonprayag and transfer to hotel.

📍 Route: Guptkashi🏨 Stay: Hotel🍽️ Meals: Breakfast / Dinner
Day 09

Guptkashi → Badrinath

Drive to Badrinath via Joshimath. Evening temple darshan subject to temple timings.

📍 Route: Badrinath🏨 Stay: Hotel in Badrinath🍽️ Meals: Breakfast / Dinner
Day 10

Badrinath → Joshimath / Rudraprayag sector

Morning darshan and proceed towards the lower Himalayan sector.

📍 Route: Rudraprayag / nearby🏨 Stay: Hotel🍽️ Meals: Breakfast / Dinner
Day 11

Return to Haridwar / Delhi sector

Complete the return transfer as per the confirmed route and drop at the designated point.

📍 Route: Haridwar / Delhi🏨 Stay: —🍽️ Meals: Breakfast
