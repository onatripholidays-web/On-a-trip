  • Kedarnath: Plan the Sonprayag/Kedarnath sector and the walking requirements.
  • Badrinath: Confirm the route and overnight stay around the Badrinath/Joshimath sector.
  • Transport: Ask what local vehicle arrangements are included.
  • Meals & hotels: Confirm the exact plan, room sharing and locations.

Pricing changes with dates, vehicle type, accommodation, group size and long-distance travel arrangements. Ask for the latest 2026 batch price and inclusions before booking.

Do Dham can be a shorter alternative to Char Dham, but Kedarnath still involves a physically demanding approach. Travellers should choose the itinerary based on mobility, walking ability, weather tolerance and available travel time.
