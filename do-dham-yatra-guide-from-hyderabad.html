Do Dham Yatra from Hyderabad: Kedarnath & Badrinath Route Guide

Do Dham Yatra is a shorter Himalayan pilgrimage focused on Kedarnath and Badrinath. It is a useful option for travellers who want the two major shrines without taking on the complete four-dham circuit.

Best time to visit

May to June and late September to October are commonly preferred. Always check current weather, road conditions and official pilgrimage arrangements before departure.

Suggested route from Hyderabad

Hyderabad → Delhi/Haridwar → Guptkashi/Sitapur → Sonprayag/Gaurikund → Kedarnath → return to the lower Himalayan route → Badrinath → Rishikesh/Haridwar → Hyderabad.

Practical planning tips

  • Choose Do Dham if you want a shorter pilgrimage than the full Char Dham circuit.
  • Keep one buffer period where possible for weather or road delays.
  • Plan the Kedarnath walking day separately rather than combining it with long road travel.
  • Carry comfortable footwear, layers and basic medicines.
  • Ask about room-sharing and meal arrangements before confirming a group package.

Frequently asked questions

Which two dhams are covered in Do Dham?

The most common Uttarakhand Do Dham circuit covers Kedarnath and Badrinath.

How many days does Do Dham take?

Road itineraries often take about 6 to 9 days depending on the starting point and rest days.

Can senior citizens travel?

Yes, with careful pacing and suitable support. A medical fitness discussion with their doctor and advance planning are sensible.
