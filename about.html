About us

Travel planned with care.

On A Trip Holidays is a Hyderabad-based travel agency specialising in customised holidays, domestic tours and spiritual journeys including Char Dham and Kedarnath.

More than a booking

We help travellers plan the complete journey — tickets, hotels, vehicles, sightseeing and itinerary coordination. Our focus is straightforward: clear communication, practical itineraries and dependable support.

Built for Telugu travellers

We support travellers from Telangana and Andhra Pradesh with Telugu-friendly assistance and group travel options.

  • Hotel & homestay arrangements
  • Private & group vehicles
  • Complete itineraries
  • Pilgrimage & darshan assistance
  • Domestic & international holidays
