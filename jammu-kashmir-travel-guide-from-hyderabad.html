Jammu & Kashmir Travel Guide from Hyderabad: Srinagar, Gulmarg & Pahalgam

Jammu & Kashmir offers very different experiences across the year: spring gardens, summer mountain escapes, autumn colours and winter snow. Most leisure itineraries focus on the Kashmir Valley, especially Srinagar, Gulmarg, Pahalgam and Sonamarg.

Best time to visit

Spring is excellent for gardens and flowers, summer is comfortable in the valley, autumn brings clear scenery and winter is popular for snow. Choose your season based on the experience you want.

Suggested route from Hyderabad

Hyderabad → Srinagar → Gulmarg → Pahalgam → Sonamarg → Srinagar → Hyderabad. Jammu can be added for pilgrimage-focused trips.

Practical planning tips

  • Keep weather and road conditions in mind for mountain day trips.
  • If snow is the priority, travel with realistic expectations because conditions vary.
  • Choose a Srinagar hotel or houseboat based on location and access.
  • Keep a little flexibility for road closures or weather.
  • For Vaishno Devi, plan Jammu/Katra separately from the Kashmir Valley circuit when possible.

Frequently asked questions

How many days are enough for Kashmir?

Six to eight days is a comfortable range for Srinagar, Gulmarg, Pahalgam and Sonamarg.

What is the best time to visit Kashmir?

The best month depends on the experience: spring for flowers, summer for sightseeing, autumn for clear scenery and winter for snow.

Can Kashmir and Vaishno Devi be combined?

Yes. A longer Jammu & Kashmir itinerary can combine Katra/Vaishno Devi with the Kashmir Valley.
