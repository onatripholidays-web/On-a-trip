Delhi Travel Guide from Hyderabad: Places, Food, Route & 3-Day Itinerary

Delhi is a strong starting point for North India travel because it combines major heritage attractions, food, markets and transport connections. It also works well as the first or last stop of a Golden Triangle or Himalayan trip.

Best time to visit

October to March is generally comfortable for extensive sightseeing, while the hotter months require shorter outdoor sessions and more indoor breaks.

Suggested route from Hyderabad

Hyderabad → Delhi → Old Delhi & Red Fort → central Delhi → Qutub Minar/Humayun’s Tomb → optional Agra/Jaipur extension → Hyderabad.

Practical planning tips

  • Start heritage sightseeing early to avoid the strongest daytime heat and crowds.
  • Use a combination of metro and local transfers depending on the route.
  • Keep food and market stops realistic within the sightseeing day.
  • Delhi can be paired with Agra and Jaipur for a Golden Triangle trip.
  • If Delhi is a transit point for Char Dham or Himachal, keep sufficient transfer buffer.

Frequently asked questions

How many days are enough for Delhi?

Two to three full days cover the main highlights at a comfortable pace.

Can Delhi be combined with Agra and Jaipur?

Yes. A 5-7 day Golden Triangle itinerary is a popular extension.

What is Delhi known for?

Delhi is known for its historic monuments, Mughal and colonial heritage, food, markets and role as a major gateway to North India.
